Expedition Two

Island by Island. Then South America.

The Rim Run Caribe™ is the second expedition of the Blue Rim 5™. It begins with the crossing from Florida into the Bahamas, then runs south through every island we can reach, including Jamaica and possibly Cuba if it is safe to do so, to Port of Spain, Trinidad.

From Trinidad the circuit turns east around South America to at least Recife, Brazil, and continues south to Rio de Janeiro, where it stops. It then returns north through Venezuela and Colombia to Panama, and through the six Western Caribbean countries.

The circuit assumes a fleet of at least three to five vessels, so that the overnight passages of island hopping can be covered.

Florida to Rio de Janeiro, and back through the Caribbean.
The Panama Rule

Shamrocket never transits the Panama Canal. Where the circuit crosses the isthmus, she portages.

Where It Fits

Expedition Two of Three.

The Rim Run Caribe™ follows the BEC and precedes the Rim Run Pac™. Together the three expeditions make up the Blue Rim 5™, departing Mobile, Alabama, November 1, 2026, and completing at Puerto Peñasco (Rocky Point), Sonora, Mexico.
