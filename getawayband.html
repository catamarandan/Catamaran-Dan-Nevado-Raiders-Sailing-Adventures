The Band

Catamaran Dan and the Getaway Band

Catamaran Dan and the Getaway Band is the sound of the Blue Rim Expedition Chronicles — original music written and produced from the deck of a 17-foot Hobie Getaway catamaran named Shamrocket, on a multi-year sailing circumnavigation of the Americas.

Every song starts the same way: a place, a person, or a moment on the route that needed to be kept. From a landlocked ranch in the White Mountains of Arizona to raft-ups hundreds of boats deep in the Eastern Caribbean, the songs carry the expedition forward — one stop, one story, one track at a time.

The Sound

Built for open water

No single genre holds the catalog down. The songs move the way the expedition moves — anchoring wherever the story calls for it.

ANTHEM

Rally & Theme

Big, singable tracks built to carry the expedition's identity — written for raft-ups, crews, and open water crowds to answer back.

BALLAD

Tribute & Reflection

Stripped-down, restrained songwriting for the quieter moments — tributes, crossings, and the weight of the miles.

BILINGUAL

Route-Driven

Spanish-language and Latin-influenced tracks that follow the western Caribbean and Latin American legs of the Rim Run.
