Season After Season

From then on, the seasons take over. Each one adds to the register, the research, and the story — and each one ends up in the Expedition Log.

The Work

What Each Season Is For.

Winters in the Bahamas. Pure snorkeling and diving, a position-tagged register of every dive site, anchorage, and uninhabited cay, field research alongside marine research teams, and promoting the Bahamas to boaters and divers.

Summers on the Florida and Atlantic side. Logging the coast from the Dry Tortugas to North Carolina, verifying hurricane holes, shipwreck history research, filming and live broadcasts, and music at the waterfront venues along the way.

Weather windows decide when we move. The quality of a place decides how long we stay.
