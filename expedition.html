Blue Rim 5™ Global Sailing Expedition

Phase One of the Blue Rim 5™ Global Sailing Expedition is the Alabama → Bahamas → Caribbean → Panama coastal progression — a disciplined, sail-first build phase designed to refine systems, strengthen seamanship, and establish the expedition culture in real water.

After reaching Panama, Catamaran Dan will remain there operating structured Panama → Belize → Panama Rim Run™ circuits beginning each January — building experience, consistency, and fleet cohesion season by season.

When the fleet is disciplined, aligned, and properly assembled, Blue Rim 5™ expands beyond the Caribbean and continues its global circumnavigation.
