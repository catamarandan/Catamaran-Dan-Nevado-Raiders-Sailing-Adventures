The Foundation

Phase 1 — The Rim Run™

The Rim Run™ is the operational foundation of Blue Rim 5™. An annual circuit aboard Shamrocket, a Hobie Getaway catamaran drawing 24 inches, sailing entirely under sail and solar power. Six countries. The full length of the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef system. One loop, every year — refined each season.

This first phase is about repetition, navigation discipline, anchoring standards, weather judgment, and operational maturity. Each coastal segment builds confidence, capability, and cohesion within the developing fleet.

Operating Philosophy

Passage Planning That Respects the Sea

The Blue Rim 5™ fleet favors daylight navigation and conservative passage planning. We do not sail at night unless conditions, safety, or geography require it to reach the next destination. Clear landfalls, rested crews, and good judgment matter more than speed or schedules.

☀️

Daylight Navigation

Every passage planned for a daylight landfall. Reef entries in good light, rested, with weather confirmed.

⚓

Anchoring Standards

One crew member always remains with the vessels. Proper scope, proper watch, proper respect for the anchorage.

🌤️

Weather Judgment

Conservative windows. The itinerary moves for weather — not the other way around. Preparation over bravado.

🗺️

Reef Respect

Shamrocket's 24-inch draft earns access to places larger boats anchor and stare at. That access is never abused.

Global Phase

When the Fleet Is Ready, Blue Rim 5™ Expands.

Catamaran Dan will operate structured Rim Run™ circuits beginning each November — strengthening routines, deepening reef immersion, and building fleet alignment season by season. When the fleet is disciplined, aligned, and properly assembled, Blue Rim 5™ expands beyond the Caribbean and continues its global circumnavigation.

The Model

Start small, learn fast, go deep, keep going. Each captain covers their own expenses. Each vessel operates independently with shared purpose and shared standards. The reefs and coastlines we're sailing are changing fast. We will not trace the planet's rim and lie to children about what's happening to it.
