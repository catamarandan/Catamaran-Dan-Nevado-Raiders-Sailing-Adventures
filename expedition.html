Blue Rim 5™ Global Sailing Expedition

A repeating annual circuit between Belize and Panama aboard Shamrocket, a Hobie Getaway catamaran. Six countries, 1,850 nautical miles, one small boat operating entirely under sail and solar power. The Rim Run™ builds seamanship, establishes the route, documents the reef systems, and forms the operational foundation for the broader Blue Rim 5™ global expedition.

This first phase is about repetition, navigation discipline, anchoring standards, weather judgment, and operational maturity. Each coastal segment builds confidence, capability, and cohesion within the developing fleet.

Catamaran Dan will operate structured Belize → Panama → Panama Rim Run™ circuits beginning each November — strengthening routines, deepening reef immersion, and building fleet alignment season by season.

When the fleet is disciplined, aligned, and properly assembled, Blue Rim 5™ expands beyond the Caribbean and continues its global circumnavigation.
