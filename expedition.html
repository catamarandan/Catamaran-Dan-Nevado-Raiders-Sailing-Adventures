What This Is

Mostly Fun. All Real.

Ninety percent of this is sailing, diving, snorkeling, exploring, eating whatever comes out of the water, and meeting people at waterfront bars who have better stories than anything on the internet. The other ten percent is paying attention — taking notes, shooting photos, noticing what's happening to the reefs — and sharing what I see.

No institution to report to. No deliverables. No one depending on me for data. Just a guy on a small boat who's been around long enough to know that these places are worth paying attention to, and who wants to document what's still here while it still is.

Shamrocket is a Hobie Getaway catamaran — 17 feet, 24-inch draft. She gets into places larger boats anchor and stare at. That's the whole point. Wind and solar. Zero carbon. Every anchorage earned.

The Bigger Picture

The BEC is the first chapter of Blue Rim 5™ — a multi-year coastal sailing expedition that continues from Puerto Aventuras into the Rim Run Caribe™ (expected January 2028) and eventually the Pacific circuit. But right now it's about the Bahamas and the Eastern Caribbean, and doing it right, one day at a time.

The Route

Four Volumes. One Continuous Journey.

The BEC runs from Palafox Pier in Pensacola all the way to Puerto Aventuras, Mexico — 377 days, 63 stops, four distinct chapters. Each volume is its own world.

Volume I

Pensacola to Bimini

Gulf Coast Florida, the Keys, Dry Tortugas, East Coast Florida, Bimini. 23 stops. The warm-up act — and it's a hell of a warm-up act.

Nov 1, 2026 – Mar 27, 2027 • 132 Days
Volume II

Bahamas & Turks and Caicos

Grand Bahama, Nassau, Andros, Eleuthera, Exumas, Long Island, Crooked Island, Mayaguana, Great Inagua, Providenciales, Grand Turk. 11 stops. The Bahamas as they were meant to be seen — from 24 inches of draft.

Mar 27 – Jun 2, 2027 • 62 Days
Volume III

Puerto Rico through Trinidad

Puerto Rico, Culebra, Vieques, USVI, BVI, St. Maarten, Antigua, Guadeloupe, Dominica, Martinique, St. Lucia, St. Vincent, Tobago Cays, Grenada, Trinidad & Tobago. 17 stops. The island chain done properly — no rush, no skipping.

Jun 2 – Sep 20, 2027 • 110 Days
Volume IV

Trinidad to Puerto Aventuras

Margarita Island, Los Roques, Bonaire, Curaçao, Cartagena, San Blas, Portobelo, Corn Islands, Roatán, Belize, Banco Chinchorro, Puerto Aventuras. 12 stops. The final run home.

Sep 20 – Dec 2, 2027 • 73 Days

What Comes Next

Arrival at Puerto Aventuras in early January 2028 marks the end of the BEC and the beginning of the next chapter — the Rim Run Caribe™, a deeper circuit through Mexico, Belize, Guatemala, and Honduras that I expect to launch around January 2028. That's when the real reef work begins in earnest. But first: 377 days of the Bahamas and the Eastern Caribbean.

The Part I'm Looking Forward To Most

Tell Me Your Story.

Find Me at the Marina. Or the Bar.

Every place I stop, there are people who know things — fishermen who've watched the reefs change over thirty years, sailors who've been coming back to the same anchorage since the eighties, dive operators who could write a book about what they've seen underwater. Locals who grew up here. Cruisers who got here and never left.

Those are the conversations I'm after. The stories the world doesn't hear because nobody asked. If you've got one — find me at the dock, pull up a chair, and let's talk. I want to know what you know. And if there's something cold in a glass, even better.

The Boat

Shamrocket — 24 Inches of Draft.

Hobie Getaway catamaran. 17 feet. 24-inch draft. Wind and solar. Torqeedo electric outboard. Starlink Mini for connectivity. Jackery Explorer 2000 v2 with 440 watts of solar. Hookah dive system for getting into the reef at any depth up to 60 feet without tanks. Garmin inReach for when the satellite is the only thing between me and a very quiet ending.

She's not a big boat. That's the point. She gets where bigger boats can't go, anchors where bigger boats can't anchor, and draws exactly the right kind of attention in every port — which usually leads to the best conversations of the day.
