Blue Rim 5™ Global Sailing Expedition

Disciplined. Sail-Only. Coastal. Engineered.

Blue Rim 5™ is a global sailing expedition built around operational precision, short coastal legs, and human connection — using small expedition sailboats supported by larger guardian vessels for safety, logistics depth, and resilience.

Mission

Blue Rim 5™ is not a cruise. It is not a charter. It is not content tourism. It is a disciplined, sail-only global sailing expedition built on real seamanship, measured risk, and coastal segmentation.

A Nevado Raiders™ Sea of Cortez shakedown season is planned for fall/early winter 2026, followed by the primary Blue Rim 5™ Global Sailing Expedition departure in May 2027.

Operational Framework

  • Sail-only primary propulsion (no reliance on engine fuel).
  • Short coastal legs wherever possible.
  • Precise LAT/LON waypoint discipline.
  • Anchoring-first, self-sufficient expedition posture.
  • No propane systems onboard.
  • Country-by-country customs and immigration planning.

Fleet Model — And / And

  • Small expedition sailboats — Hobie Getaway, Catalina 22 Sport, and similar vessels.
  • Larger guardian vessels — sailing and power boats traveling with the expedition to increase safety margin, provide logistics support, medical redundancy, retrieval capability, and real-world operational depth.

Agility and seamanship at the core. Guardian vessels providing resilience, redundancy, and capability when conditions demand it.

Climate Reality

The oceans are warming. Reefs are changing. Coastlines are shifting. Blue Rim 5™ moves through the world with respect for ecosystems under measurable stress, documenting what is observed and speaking plainly about what is real.

This expedition is built on truth, discipline, and responsibility — not theatrics.
