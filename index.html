Route Discipline

The planning standard is simple: keep legs short whenever possible, favor coasts and islands, and plan for real‑world execution. Each major schedule build is approached as daily, operational entries — with precise coordinates where required.

Tracking + Emergency

Garmin inReach Explorer is part of the expedition toolset for tracking and SOS — an additional layer of accountability and safety beyond standard marine communications.
