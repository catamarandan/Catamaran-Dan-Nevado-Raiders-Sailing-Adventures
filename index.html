How the Fleet Works

Blue Rim 5™ is built around an inclusive “and/and” fleet: small expedition boats (Hobie Getaway, small monohulls, and similar vessels) supported by larger guardian vessels (sailing and power). The goal is resilience, safety, and operational realism.

Planning Standard

Sail-first. Coastal segmentation. Short legs whenever possible. Precise LAT/LON routing for waypoint creation. When entering a new country, customs/immigration check-in details are documented as part of the operating plan.

DisciplineShort segments, conservative routing, real decision-making.
CapabilitySystems, redundancy, and readiness over hype.
CulturePeace, human connection, and shared responsibility.

Timeline

Sea of Cortez shakedown in fall/early winter 2026 • Main departure May 2027.

What Happens First

The Sea of Cortez shakedown cruise is planned for fall/early winter 2026 to validate systems, routines, and real-world expedition rhythm. The Blue Rim 5™ Global Sailing Expedition departs in May 2027.

Rim Run™ Concept

Panama ↔ Belize and back — a repeating reef-measured year, lived from anchorages.

What Rim Run™ Is

Rim Run™ isn’t a charter. It isn’t a cruise. It’s expedition living built around immersion: reefs, turquoise water, coastal towns, and the daily rhythm of seamanship.

Daily Reality

Swim before coffee. Reset anchors when weather shifts. Provision like an operator. Live with wind and tide — and earn the sunsets.

ImmersionReef water, snorkeling days, and real coastal life.
ExecutionDisciplined routing and short legs whenever possible.
HumanShared experience, cultural exchange, and connection.

The Operating Model

Each participating boat/owner covers their own expenses at this stage (food, local fees, guides, activities, fuel, etc.). This may evolve in the future if sponsorship or additional funding becomes available.

Operational Systems

Safety, comms, navigation discipline, energy and water — the practical realities.

Comms + Tracking

Garmin inReach Explorer is part of the toolset for tracking and SOS. Combined with marine VHF procedures and conservative routing, this adds accountability and safety beyond standard communications.

Planning Rules

Sail-first propulsion and coastal segmentation are core standards. Keep legs short. Avoid long offshore runs unless unavoidable. LAT/LON precision is maintained for waypoint entry in Navionics.

RedundancySystems-driven decisions; reduce single points of failure.
ReadinessProcedures, checklists, and real-world contingencies.
DisciplineConservative plans; underpromise, overdeliver.

Energy + Daily Life

Operational decisions prioritize practical reliability on a small expedition platform: what works at anchor, what works in squalls, and what stays maintainable over long durations.
