The Core Idea

Not a Scaled-Down Version of Big-Boat Cruising.

The small cat platform is built around a simple idea: use a lightweight, shallow-draft sailing catamaran as the primary vessel and pair it with a modular equipment sled carrying the essential systems needed for coastal living, travel, and exploration. The result is a system that stays close to shore, keeps passages short, reaches places larger vessels cannot, and lowers the barrier to ownership and long-term use.

Access

A 24-inch draft opens up reef zones, sandbars, protected shallows, and remote coastal areas that deeper boats cannot safely reach. Shamrocket anchors directly over shallow reef structures that dive boats cannot approach — placing the science platform on top of sites that rarely see in-water observation.

Simplicity

Sail-driven movement, fewer major systems, and reduced dependence on fuel and marina infrastructure create a cleaner, more practical operating model. Fewer systems mean fewer failure points — critical for sustained multi-year operation in remote coastal environments.

Repeatability

Lower cost, lower complexity, and a modular support system make long-term repeat use realistic. The Rim Run™ returns to the same 100+ sites every year. That continuity is the scientific value of the platform — it builds over time.

The goal is not to imitate large-vessel cruising. The goal is to create the right platform for this mission — and to keep coming back.
Platform Advantages

Why a Small Sailing Catamaran Works.

  • No primary fuel requirement — sail-driven propulsion reduces cost, logistics, and dependence on fuel availability. Zero carbon in operation.
  • Extremely shallow draft — 24 inches of draft provides access to waters and shorelines that larger boats often cannot use. Essential for reef-edge science work.
  • Fast and agile — well suited to short coastal legs and quick repositioning between reef sites.
  • Beachable and nearshore capable — useful for landing, gear transfer, and operating close to reefs and shoreline communities.
  • Lower purchase and maintenance cost — makes long-term ownership and multi-year commitment realistic without the financial burden of a large cruising platform.
  • Lower insurance burden — generally easier and less expensive to insure than a full-size cruising vessel.
  • Trailerable and transportable — can be broken down, containerized, shipped, and reassembled in another region without requiring an ocean crossing. Blue Rim 5™ can move beyond the Caribbean without Shamrocket needing to cross oceans to get there.
Scientific Payload

What Shamrocket Carries for the Mission.

As GROAN Level 1, Shamrocket carries a disciplined scientific payload designed for repeatable, multi-year reef observation. Every instrument feeds data directly into the GROAN data architecture. The baseline kit keeps the mission operational continuously — support and partnerships expand what can be measured without changing what the platform fundamentally is.

Baseline Environmental Instruments

Apera PCO60-Z multiparameter meter (surface air temperature, subsurface temperature, pH, salinity, ORP). DIY Secchi disk for visibility. Every site, every visit, every year.

Aerial Platform — FAA Drone

FAA-licensed UAV operations for repeat photogrammetry, bleaching documentation, seagrass and mangrove mapping, and annual visual comparison of shallow coastal habitats. Operators must be licensed. This one is.

Subsurface — SCUBA / Snorkel

Snorkel-based observation is the operational baseline. Reef Check EcoDiver SCUBA certification adds reef wall documentation, deeper fish community surveys, and benthic assessment at depth. Scheduled: Bocas del Toro, August 2026.

Visual Documentation

Underwater camera for standardized wide-angle, close-up, and transect-style video and photography at every site. Visual record preserved for annual comparison and independent validation.

Navigation & Data Logging

GPS-enabled device for permanent site coordinates. Standardized logging format capturing all measurement parameters. Every observation is site-indexed, timestamped, and structured for long-term comparison.

Priority Expansion — Dissolved Oxygen

Optical DO sensor is the highest-priority expansion instrument. Hypoxic events in Western Caribbean lagoons have caused mass coral mortality misattributed to temperature because DO goes unmeasured. Added when instrument support is secured.

The Sled System

What Changes the Equation.

The sled is what transforms the catamaran from a day-sailing setup into a true coastal expedition platform. Instead of forcing the catamaran to carry every heavy or bulky system onboard, the modular sled carries essential support gear while the sailing platform remains light, efficient, and capable.

What the Sled Carries

  • Water and provisions — reserve capacity for coastal movement and short-duration independence.
  • Electrical power — modular power systems and solar charging support.
  • Cooking systems — compact galley capability for real daily living.
  • Cold storage — small refrigeration support where needed.
  • Shelter and camp gear — sleeping, shade, and privacy systems.
  • Scientific instruments — all measurement tools, logging equipment, and data systems.
  • Fishing and expedition gear — anchors, rods, safety gear, and other essentials.

Why It Matters

The sled allows the primary sailing platform to stay simple while still supporting real coastal life. This makes the overall system practical for two adults and potentially workable for a small family within a disciplined coastal operating model.

It becomes a true coastal exploration system with room for power, gear, food, water, and the full scientific payload — without compromising the performance and simplicity that make the catamaran the right tool.

Small cat plus modular sled is not a workaround. It is the operating model.
Safety Model

Coastal Discipline. Not Offshore Ambition.

This platform is built around coastal discipline. The model depends on short sailing legs, daily landing or anchoring, early arrivals, and constant awareness of nearby shelter options. A 200-Ton Master with nuclear submarine qualification does not take unnecessary risks. The platform stays in its operational envelope.

How the Model Stays Practical

  • Short coastal legs — reduced exposure compared with long offshore passages.
  • Daily arrival model — landing or anchoring each day keeps options open.
  • Quick weather response — the platform remains close to shore and closer to shelter.
  • Shallow-water escape options — access to thin water creates alternatives not available to deeper boats.
  • Operational simplicity — fewer major systems mean fewer major failures.

What This Does Not Claim

No vessel removes risk. Seamanship, judgment, preparation, and conservative operation still matter. The point is that within a disciplined coastal envelope, a small catamaran operated by a qualified mariner is a practical and effective platform for exploration, science, and daily life.

The operator holds a USCG 200-Ton Master license, USN submarine qualification, and has been operating on the water professionally for decades. The platform is capable. The operator is qualified. The envelope is understood.

Affordability

Affordability Changes Everything.

One of the biggest strengths of this model is that it lowers the barrier to entry. Many people will never own a large cruising yacht. That does not mean they should be excluded from meaningful time on the water — or from conducting real science.

Lower Purchase Cost

Boats in this category are dramatically less expensive than full-size cruising vessels. The financial barrier to sustained coastal expedition life is not what most people assume.

Lower Operating Cost

Less dependence on fuel, marina infrastructure, and complex systems makes multi-year operation realistic on a practical budget. Zero carbon means zero fuel cost.

Lower Ownership Burden

Insurance, maintenance, and logistics are far more manageable than large-boat cruising. The platform sustains itself. The science continues regardless of sponsorship.

Affordability is not a side benefit. It is one of the main reasons this platform matters — and one of the reasons the Rim Run™ can make a 20-year commitment.
Global Mobility

One Region at a Time. One Container at a Time.

A major advantage of this type of platform is that it does not need to cross every ocean it explores. After covering one region thoroughly, it can be disassembled, containerized, shipped, and reassembled elsewhere. Blue Rim 5™ begins in the Caribbean. It does not end there.

Why That Matters

This opens the door to exploring many different coastlines without taking on unnecessary transoceanic passages. It saves time, lowers risk, and keeps the platform aligned with its real purpose: coastal exploration and sustained reef observation where the data is most needed.

What It Enables

Shamrocket can move from the Caribbean to the Indo-Pacific, the Red Sea, or any other coast without requiring an entirely different class of vessel. The same proven platform, the same scientific systems, a different region — and a growing comparative dataset that no single-deployment program can match.
