Why Support Matters

Built to Continue. Designed to Grow.

The Rim Run™ and Blue Rim 5™ observation model are built around continuity. The baseline mission is designed to stay active using a disciplined, repeatable operating model funded at the core by Catamaran Dan himself.

Support, sponsorship, and direct contributions make it possible to expand the mission in practical ways: more reef sites, stronger documentation, better instrumentation, improved data quality, wider outreach, stronger collaboration with scientists and institutions, and reduced participation barriers for qualified citizen scientists.

Beyond the expedition itself, support also fuels two permanent programs — the Caribbean Coastal Reef Alliance and The Reef Keepers — that will operate long after any single circuit is complete.
