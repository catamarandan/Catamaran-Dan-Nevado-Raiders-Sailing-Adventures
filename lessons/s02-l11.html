Data Collection

Field methods (L10) govern a single station visit. Data collection governs the entire campaign — how stations are chosen, how records are structured, how gaps are handled, and how the resulting dataset earns the right to feed a GROAN Decision Score.

Lecture
EN

A single station visit, however perfectly executed, is an anecdote. A systematically designed data collection campaign is evidence. The difference lies in four decisions made before Shamrocket leaves port: what collection mode governs the campaign design, how stations are selected and distributed, what data record schema ensures completeness, and how gaps and missing data are handled without corrupting the CMIE's Source Arbitration layer. GROAN's Caribbean proof-of-concept case study depends entirely on answering these four questions correctly for the Rim Run™ 2026 dataset.

Synoptic Survey Rim Run™ Primary Mode
A single-pass spatial survey covering all stations within a defined time window. Captures a synoptic snapshot — all stations sampled under comparable seasonal and atmospheric conditions. Rim Run™ executes as a synoptic survey: 100+ stations across 7 countries in 6–8 weeks, October–November 2026. Limitation: no temporal repeat — diel and tidal dynamics (L05) must be normalized rather than directly observed at each station.
→ GROAN: first Caribbean chemical baseline; feeds GRIN spatial layer
Moored Time-Series Key Station Anchors
Continuous data from fixed sensors at selected stations — captures diel cycles, tidal forcing, and event pulses. High temporal resolution, zero spatial coverage. GROAN designates 5–8 priority moored stations in the Rim Run™ circuit — one per country minimum, plus the Motagua plume corridor. Moored data provides the temporal context that normalizes the synoptic survey readings.
→ GROAN: diel normalization anchor; CMIE event detection layer
Transect Sampling Cross-Gradient Coverage
Stations arranged along a defined transect — typically shore-to-reef or river-to-open-ocean. Captures chemical gradients across the reef–lagoon–mangrove continuum (S02-L03 architecture). At each country stop, GROAN executes a minimum 3-station transect: mangrove fringe (GMIN), lagoon (GMAIN), and reef crest (GRIN). This is the minimum dataset that activates CMIE cross-module reasoning at each site.
→ GROAN: cross-module gradient; activates CMIE at each site
Citizen Science (CCRA) Network Extension
Caribbean Coastal Reef Alliance partners collect standardized chemical readings using the MOOP protocol between Rim Run™ transits. Extends spatial and temporal coverage beyond Shamrocket's physical reach. CCRA data enters GROAN at Moderate confidence by default — protocol standardization and training level determine whether individual contributors can achieve High confidence tier status.
→ GROAN: gap-fill layer; CCRA stations enter as Source 003+

Station selection criteria. Stations are not chosen arbitrarily. GROAN's Rim Run™ station network is designed around three selection rules: (1) Ecological representativeness — each reef type (fringing, barrier, atoll, patch) and each ecosystem zone (mangrove, seagrass, lagoon, reef crest) must be represented in every country. (2) Chemical sensitivity — priority weighting to sites known or suspected to be near chemical thresholds (Ω <3.0, adjacent to river discharge, downstream of coastal development). (3) Logistics feasibility — Shamrocket's draft (~0.6 m hulls) enables access to shallow mangrove channels inaccessible to larger vessels; stations are selected to exploit this access advantage.

GROAN station record schema — mandatory fields. Every data record submitted to the GROAN API Registry must be complete to the following schema. Partial records are retained in the raw archive but do not enter the CMIE until missing fields are resolved or explicitly assigned a "not collected" flag with justification.

GROAN Station Record — Required Fields
Station ID
Auto-assigned. Format: RR26-[country]-[seq]. e.g. RR26-BZ-014
GPS Coordinates
Decimal degrees ±0.0001°. Logged at anchor drop, not estimated.
UTC Timestamp
ISO 8601. GPS-synced. Local time recorded separately in notes.
Tidal Phase
Flood / Ebb / Slack. Hours from nearest high water. TPXO-derived.
pH
ISFET reading ±0.01. Calibration slope logged. T-compensated.
Total Alkalinity
Titration result µmol/kg. Duplicate flag if 1-in-5 station.
DO
mg/L optical. Saturation % computed. Supersaturation flag if >110%.
Salinity + Temp
CTD sonde. PSU and °C. Depth of reading logged.
Depth Profile
Required at GMIN/GMAIN stations. 3-depth min. Surface/mid/bottom.
Nutrients (N, P)
Colorimetric field kit. Required at GMIN/GMAIN. Optional at GRIN.
Ω / DIC / pCO₂
CO2SYS calculated from pH + TA. Populated in post-processing.
Confidence Tier
Auto-assigned at edge node: High / Moderate / Low. Rationale logged.
Ecosystem Zone
GRIN / GMAIN / GMIN / GSIN. Determines CMIE module routing.
Observer
MOOP (Shamrocket) or CCRA partner ID. Affects default confidence weight.
Site Notes
Free text. Disturbance flags, visible runoff, bloom, turbidity, gear issues.
SGD Flag
Boolean. SGD-influenced sites tagged for GRIN baseline correction.

Handling gaps and missing data. No 100+ station campaign produces a complete dataset. GROAN's data collection protocol distinguishes three gap types with different responses: Equipment failure — sensor data absent for a parameter; flag as "not collected — equipment," retain all other parameters, down-tier confidence. Protocol violation — tidal phase not recorded, headspace contamination suspected; flag as "not collected — protocol error," down-tier to Low regardless of parameter count. Environmental exclusion — site inaccessible due to weather, reef closure, or safety; flag as "station not attempted," no confidence tier assigned, exclude from spatial coverage map without bias.

ES

Una sola visita a una estación, por perfectamente ejecutada que sea, es una anécdota. Una campaña de recopilación de datos sistemáticamente diseñada es evidencia. La diferencia reside en cuatro decisiones tomadas antes de que Shamrocket salga del puerto: qué modo de recopilación rige el diseño de la campaña, cómo se seleccionan y distribuyen las estaciones, qué esquema de registro de datos garantiza la integridad, y cómo se manejan las brechas sin corromper la capa de Arbitraje de Fuentes del CMIE.

El Rim Run™ 2026 opera como una encuesta sinóptica primaria — más de 100 estaciones en 7 países en 6–8 semanas, con datos de series temporales ancladas en 5–8 estaciones prioritarias para anclar la normalización diurna. En cada parada de país, GROAN ejecuta un transecto mínimo de 3 estaciones: franja de manglar (GMIN), laguna (GMAIN) y cresta arrecifal (GRIN) — el conjunto de datos mínimo que activa el razonamiento cruzado del CMIE en cada sitio. La Red de Arrecifes Costeros del Caribe (CCRA) extiende la cobertura como capa de relleno de brechas entre los tránsitos de Shamrocket, entrando al Registro API de GROAN como Confianza Moderada por defecto.

El esquema de registro de estación GROAN define 16 campos: 8 requeridos (ID de estación, GPS, marca de tiempo UTC, fase de marea, pH, alcalinidad total, DO, salinidad+temperatura), 3 condicionales (perfil de profundidad, nutrientes, Ω/CID/pCO₂), y 5 de metadatos (nivel de confianza, zona de ecosistema, observador, notas del sitio, indicador SGD). Los registros parciales se retienen en el archivo bruto pero no entran al CMIE hasta que los campos faltantes se resuelvan o se les asigne explícitamente un indicador "no recopilado" con justificación.

Application
EN
  • The 3-station minimum transect rule (GMIN → GMAIN → GRIN at each country stop) is the data collection decision that makes the Caribbean proof-of-concept case study scientifically defensible. Without all three, CMIE cannot calculate the cross-ecosystem chemical gradient that distinguishes GROAN from a single-module monitoring program — the transect is where the thesis argument becomes measurable.
  • Moored time-series stations at the Motagua plume corridor and at one Belizean barrier reef station are the two highest-priority fixed deployments in the Rim Run™ circuit — they provide the continuous record of flood pulse arrivals, diel pH cycling amplitude, and seasonal baseline shift that contextualizes every synoptic survey reading within 200 km of that corridor.
  • CCRA partner data at Moderate confidence default is not a weakness — it is a strategic design choice. GROAN explicitly builds a two-tier data system: Shamrocket's MOOP provides High-confidence anchors; CCRA partners extend spatial coverage at known lower precision. The Source Arbitration layer weights these proportionally rather than excluding the lower tier, maximizing information content while accurately representing uncertainty.
  • The SGD flag in the station record schema (L10 field method context) is a data collection decision, not just a metadata label — it determines whether a High-confidence Ω reading from a karst-influenced site is used as a regional reef benchmark or quarantined as a geological outlier in GRIN's baseline construction.
  • For the CORDAP grant application context: GROAN's 16-field station record schema, standardized across 100+ stations in 7 countries, is the dataset architecture that makes CORDAP's regional synthesis possible. No existing Caribbean reef chemistry dataset covers this geographic range with this parameter suite and this documentation standard simultaneously.
ES
  • La regla de transecto mínimo de 3 estaciones (GMIN → GMAIN → GRIN en cada parada de país) es la decisión de recopilación de datos que hace científicamente defendible el estudio de caso de prueba de concepto del Caribe. Sin las tres, el CMIE no puede calcular el gradiente químico entre ecosistemas que distingue a GROAN de un programa de monitoreo de módulo único.
  • Las estaciones de series temporales ancladas en el corredor de la pluma del Motagua y en una estación de arrecife de barrera de Belice son los dos despliegues fijos de mayor prioridad en el circuito Rim Run™ — proporcionan el registro continuo de llegadas de pulsos de crecida, amplitud de ciclo de pH diurno, y cambio de línea de base estacional que contextualiza cada lectura de la encuesta sinóptica dentro de 200 km de ese corredor.
  • Los datos de los socios de la CCRA con Confianza Moderada por defecto no son una debilidad — es una elección de diseño estratégico. GROAN construye explícitamente un sistema de datos de dos niveles: MOOP de Shamrocket proporciona anclas de Alta Confianza; los socios de la CCRA extienden la cobertura espacial con menor precisión conocida. La capa de Arbitraje de Fuentes los pondera proporcionalmente en lugar de excluir el nivel inferior.
  • El indicador SGD en el esquema de registro de estación es una decisión de recopilación de datos, no solo una etiqueta de metadatos — determina si una lectura de Ω de Alta Confianza de un sitio influenciado por karst se usa como referencia arrecifal regional o se pone en cuarentena como valor atípico geológico en la construcción de la línea de base de GRIN.
  • Para el contexto de la solicitud de subvención de CORDAP: el esquema de registro de estación de 16 campos de GROAN, estandarizado en más de 100 estaciones en 7 países, es la arquitectura de datos que hace posible la síntesis regional de CORDAP. Ningún conjunto de datos existente de química de arrecifes del Caribe cubre este rango geográfico con esta suite de parámetros y este estándar de documentación simultáneamente.
Assessment · Evaluación
QUESTION 01
Why does GROAN require a minimum 3-station transect (GMIN → GMAIN → GRIN) at every Rim Run™ country stop rather than simply collecting the highest number of reef crest readings possible?
¿Por qué GROAN requiere un transecto mínimo de 3 estaciones (GMIN → GMAIN → GRIN) en cada parada de país del Rim Run™ en lugar de simplemente recopilar el mayor número posible de lecturas de cresta arrecifal?
  • A Because international reef monitoring standards require tri-habitat sampling for certification
  • B To ensure sufficient replication for statistical significance within each country
  • C Because the CMIE requires cross-ecosystem chemical gradient data to generate causal reasoning — without all three zones, GROAN functions as a single-module monitor, not a decision system
  • D To maximize the number of stations that qualify for High confidence tier
ANSWER · RESPUESTA: C
QUESTION 02
A CCRA citizen science partner submits a station record with pH, DO, salinity, and temperature — but no total alkalinity, no tidal phase, and no GPS coordinates. What happens to this record in GROAN?
Un socio de ciencia ciudadana de la CCRA envía un registro de estación con pH, DO, salinidad y temperatura — pero sin alcalinidad total, sin fase de marea y sin coordenadas GPS. ¿Qué sucede con este registro en GROAN?
  • A It is rejected entirely and removed from the archive
  • B It enters the CMIE at Moderate confidence since three parameters were collected
  • C It is retained in the raw archive at Low confidence — missing TA prevents Ω calculation, missing tidal phase prevents dynamic contextualization, and missing GPS prevents spatial assignment. It does not enter the CMIE until fields are resolved.
  • D It is automatically upgraded to High confidence because CCRA partners use certified protocols
ANSWER · RESPUESTA: C
QUESTION 03
What distinguishes an "equipment failure gap" from a "protocol violation gap" in GROAN's missing data handling, and why does the distinction matter?
¿Qué distingue una "brecha por falla de equipo" de una "brecha por violación de protocolo" en el manejo de datos faltantes de GROAN, y por qué es importante la distinción?
  • A Equipment failures produce more missing parameters, so they always result in lower confidence scores
  • B Equipment failures are random and uninformative about data quality — remaining parameters are still valid; protocol violations indicate systematic errors that may have compromised all parameters at that station, justifying auto-Low regardless of parameter count
  • C Protocol violations are always correctable in post-processing; equipment failures are not
  • D The distinction only matters for CCRA data, not for MOOP data collected aboard Shamrocket
ANSWER · RESPUESTA: B
