Monitoring Methods

In-situ sensors, discrete sampling, remote sensing — instrumentation, calibration chains, and GROAN confidence tier assignment

Lecture
EN — English

GROAN's chemical data quality is bounded at the bottom by the weakest instrument in the collection chain. A CMIE attribution that generates a defensible Decision Score depends on every input arriving with a documented confidence tier — and that tier is determined at the point of measurement, not during analysis. Method selection is a confidence tier decision. Choosing the wrong sensor for a given parameter, failing to calibrate on schedule, or mishandling a discrete sample does not produce uncertain data — it produces data whose uncertainty is unknown, which is structurally worse than a documented Moderate-confidence reading.

GROAN's monitoring methods organize into three tiers:

High-eligible · In-situ Sensors
  • ISFET pH sensor Gold standard for reef in-situ pH. Resolution ±0.001 units. Requires spectrophotometric recalibration every 72 hours of deployment. Beyond that window without recalibration: automatic Moderate confidence.
  • CTD (Conductivity-Temperature-Depth) Foundation measurement — salinity, temperature, density, depth. Required at every MOOP station. All carbonate calculations are temperature- and salinity-dependent; no CTD record = no valid carbonate calculation.
  • DO Optode (fluorescence-based) Preferred over Clark electrodes for multi-day deployment — lower drift, no electrolyte consumption. Biofouling on membrane suppresses readings; inspection and cleaning mandatory every 24 hours in reef zones.
  • SAMI-CO₂ / pCO₂ sensor Autonomous underway pCO₂ measurement. Equilibrator-based; requires temperature correction for sea-surface pCO₂ calculation. High confidence when CTD co-deployed.
High-eligible · Discrete Sampling
  • Total Alkalinity (TA) — open-cell potentiometric titration Reference method, ±1 µmol/kg precision. Samples must be preserved with mercuric chloride (HgCl₂) within 4 hours of collection and stored at 4°C. Samples outside this window are discarded from the CMIE record — not downgraded.
  • DIC — coulometric analysis Lab-only method, turnaround 2–4 weeks. TA + DIC pair fully constrains the carbonate system. Field collection; lab analysis; High confidence when certified reference material (CRM) batch correction applied.
  • Nutrient colorimetry (DIN, SRP) Field-deployable for DIN and SRP. N:P ratio computed from co-collected DIN and SRP at same station. Reagent stability degrades above 30°C — carry cold storage for reagents aboard Shamrocket.
  • Duplicate sampling protocol Duplicate samples collected at every 5th corridor station. If duplicates diverge by >2% for TA or >0.01 pH units, flag the entire station record and re-sample before departing the site.
Moderate floor · Remote & Reanalysis
  • Sentinel-2 (CDOM, turbidity, habitat) 10 m spatial resolution. CDOM proxy valid only when Secchi depth >3 m. Turbid zones (Secchi <3 m) produce unreliable CDOM retrievals — turbidity flag check mandatory before any S-2 CDOM value upgrades station confidence. ±3 day overpass alignment with MOOP station required.
  • GLORYS regional reanalysis (1/12°, ~8 km) Temperature, salinity, current fields. Temporal anchor for corridor aliasing correction. Minimum viable downscaling product for GROAN spatial attribution. Does not resolve reef-zone chemistry — requires MOOP anchor.
  • Ra-222 SGD detector Identifies submarine groundwater discharge by dissolved radon anomaly. Mandatory at all MAR lagoon stations; discretionary elsewhere triggered by salinity anomaly. Turnaround: field-deployable, 4–8 hour count time.
  • δ¹⁵N / δ¹³C / δ¹⁸O (IRMS — lab) Stable isotope source attribution. Lab only, 4–6 week turnaround. Required for agricultural vs. karst DIN partitioning in MAR. Samples collected in field; analysis outsourced. Results classified as Moderate confidence until IRMS batch QC confirmed.

The calibration chain. Every GROAN chemical value follows a documented path from reference standard to CMIE input:

NIST / CRM Reference Standard → Lab Instrument Calibration → Field Sensor Cal. Check → MOOP Collection (REV-compliant) → L3 Source Arbitration → CMIE Input

A break at any link in this chain does not just affect that link — it propagates forward as an undocumented uncertainty into every CMIE attribution that uses the affected value. A sensor calibrated against a lab instrument that was itself out of CRM compliance produces a High-labeled value with Moderate-or-worse actual uncertainty. GROAN's L3 Source Arbitration exists partly to catch these chain breaks through recency weighting and confidence cross-checks before CMIE consumes the data.

Zero-carbon constraint and method selection. Shamrocket is a sailing catamaran with no chase vessel and limited cold-storage capacity. This shapes method selection directly: reagent-dependent colorimetric methods require cold storage; HgCl₂-preserved TA samples require a secure hazardous materials protocol aboard; IRMS samples require chain of custody from collection to lab submission at country ports. Methods that cannot be executed reliably from a sailing platform under expedition conditions are not High-confidence methods for GROAN — they are Moderate-confidence by operational constraint, regardless of their laboratory precision specification.

ES — Español

La calidad de los datos químicos de GROAN está limitada en su límite inferior por el instrumento más débil en la cadena de recolección. La selección del método es una decisión de nivel de confianza. Elegir el sensor equivocado para un parámetro dado, no calibrarlo según el cronograma, o manejar incorrectamente una muestra discreta no produce datos inciertos — produce datos cuya incertidumbre es desconocida, lo que es estructuralmente peor que una lectura de confianza Moderada documentada.

Sensores in situ (High elegible): El sensor ISFET de pH es el estándar de oro con resolución ±0.001 unidades, requiriendo recalibración espectrofotométrica cada 72 horas. El CTD es la medición fundamental — sin registro CTD no hay cálculo de carbonatos válido. El optode de OD requiere inspección y limpieza cada 24 horas en zonas de arrecife para prevenir el ensuciamiento de la membrana.

Muestreo discreto (High elegible): Las muestras de Alcalinidad Total deben preservarse con cloruro de mercurio (HgCl₂) dentro de 4 horas de la recolección y almacenarse a 4°C. Las muestras fuera de esta ventana se descartan — no se degradan. Se recolectan muestras duplicadas en cada 5ª estación del corredor; si los duplicados divergen más del 2% para TA o 0.01 unidades de pH, se marca el registro completo de la estación.

Métodos remotos y de reanálisis (piso Moderado): El proxy CDOM de Sentinel-2 solo es válido cuando la profundidad Secchi es >3 m. GLORYS proporciona el ancla temporal para la corrección de aliasing del corredor. Ra-222 es obligatorio en todas las estaciones lagunares del MAR. Los isótopos estables (δ¹⁵N, δ¹³C) tienen un tiempo de respuesta de 4–6 semanas en laboratorio.

Restricción de cero carbono: Shamrocket es un catamarán de vela sin embarcación de apoyo y capacidad limitada de almacenamiento en frío. Esto influye directamente en la selección de métodos — los métodos que no pueden ejecutarse de manera confiable desde una plataforma de vela bajo condiciones de expedición son métodos de confianza Moderada por restricción operacional, independientemente de su especificación de precisión de laboratorio.

Application
EN — English
  • 01ISFET pH sensors deployed for more than 72 hours without recalibration against a spectrophotometric reference must have all readings from that period downgraded to Moderate confidence before L3 Source Arbitration. The drift accumulates non-linearly — a sensor at hour 80 is not 10% less accurate than at hour 72; it may have drifted into a different error regime entirely. The 72-hour window is a hard threshold, not a guideline.
  • 02Inspect and clean DO optode membranes every 24 hours in reef zones. Biofouling in warm, productive shallow water accumulates faster than in open ocean deployments — a fouled membrane suppresses DO readings and will produce false hypoxia flags in CMIE. If a 24-hour inspection cycle cannot be maintained during a passage leg, flag all DO readings from that period as potentially fouled and assign Moderate confidence regardless of instrument diagnostics.
  • 03Preserve all TA and DIC discrete samples with HgCl₂ within 4 hours of collection. Samples not preserved within this window are discarded — not downgraded to Moderate. Biological activity continues in unpreserved samples and will alter TA by measurable amounts within hours in warm reef water. Partial preservation data are not usable as Moderate-confidence inputs; they are unusable. Carry adequate HgCl₂ stock for the full corridor count before departure.
  • 04Before using any Sentinel-2 CDOM proxy value to upgrade a corridor station from Moderate to High confidence, confirm Secchi depth exceeds 3 m at the station. In turbid zones — particularly near river plume boundaries in Guatemala and Honduras — CDOM retrieval algorithms produce systematically biased outputs. The turbidity check is not a post-hoc quality filter; it must be performed before the upgrade decision is made. A station in a turbid zone remains at Moderate confidence regardless of how many Sentinel-2 overpasses are available.
  • 05Collect duplicate samples at every 5th MOOP corridor station. If duplicates diverge by more than 2% for TA or 0.01 pH units, do not leave the site without re-sampling. A failed duplicate check indicates either a collection error or an REV violation — both require resolution before the station enters the CMIE record. If re-sampling is operationally impossible (weather, vessel constraint), mark the station as single-sample unconfirmed and assign Moderate confidence for all parameters collected there.
