Human Impacts

Anthropogenic chemical pathways — land use, tourism, sewage, aquaculture, maritime — and δ¹⁵N source attribution

Lecture
EN — English

Human impacts translate to reef chemistry through six distinct pathways, each with a diagnostic chemical signature, a required GROAN attribution protocol, and — in several cases — a documented data gap that must be explicitly flagged in the CMIE calibration record. The critical distinction between this lesson and the stressors taxonomy (L25) is attribution: identifying the human activity responsible for a chemical anomaly, not just measuring the anomaly. CMIE's intervention target is only as actionable as its attribution is specific. A Decision Score that names eutrophication as the stressor without naming the specific human activity driving it — agriculture, sewage, or aquaculture — cannot direct intervention to the right sector.

Impact 01 · Agriculture
DIN ↑ N:P >16 δ¹⁵N 8–12‰ Wet season pulse

Sugarcane, citrus, banana, and palm cultivation in the MAR watershed countries apply nitrogen fertilizer at intensities of 80–200 kg N/ha/year, generating DIN loading that pulses into reef zones during wet season runoff events. The agricultural N:P ratio typically exceeds 16 — reflecting fertilizer formulations optimized for nitrogen. Stable nitrogen isotope signature δ¹⁵N of 8–12‰ distinguishes agricultural DIN from karst groundwater (2–5‰) and sewage (10–20‰). Land use maps combined with discharge plume tracking are required for CMIE to identify the specific watershed driving the reef zone DIN signal.

Impact 02 · Coastal Development
Impervious ↑ Runoff pulse Heavy metals DATA GAP

Expansion of impervious surfaces — roads, buildings, parking areas — increases runoff coefficients and concentrates stormwater chemistry. Urban runoff carries heavy metals (zinc, lead, copper from vehicles and roofing), hydrocarbons, and microplastics to coastal waters. Construction sites mobilize sediment that suppresses light and buries coral. GROAN's standard MOOP kit does not include heavy metal or hydrocarbon analysis — this is an active data gap for reef zones adjacent to rapidly developing tourism infrastructure in Belize and Honduras. Flag in CMIE calibration record for any station within 5 km of active construction or urban expansion.

Impact 03 · Tourism & Recreation
Oxybenzone Octinoxate sub-ppb toxic DATA GAP

Chemical sunscreen active ingredients — oxybenzone (benzophenone-3) and octinoxate — are endocrine disruptors in coral at concentrations as low as 62 parts per trillion, causing coral bleaching, DNA damage, and larval deformation. High-traffic dive sites and swim beaches in Mexico, Belize, and Honduras receive loading that may exceed toxic thresholds during peak tourism season. Targeted GC-MS organic chemistry is required for detection — outside MOOP standard kit. Any reef zone receiving >500 recreational visitors per year with no oxybenzone or octinoxate data must be flagged as potentially understating bleaching risk in the CMIE record, independent of all other chemical indicators.

Impact 04 · Wastewater & Sewage
NH₄⁺ signature δ¹⁵N 10–20‰ Pharmaceuticals

Direct sewage discharge to coastal waters is the dominant DIN source at several Belizean cayes and Honduran coastal communities lacking municipal treatment infrastructure. The sewage DIN signature is primarily ammonium (NH₄⁺) rather than nitrate — distinguishable by speciation analysis — with δ¹⁵N of 10–20‰, elevated above agricultural values. Pharmaceutical compounds including synthetic estrogens, antibiotics, and anti-inflammatories enter reefs through sewage and act as endocrine disruptors at sub-µg/L concentrations. Co-occurring sewage and agricultural signals require a multi-endmember δ¹⁵N mixing model — simple two-endmember attribution is insufficient when both sources are present simultaneously.

Impact 05 · Aquaculture
DIN · SRP ↑ δ¹⁵N 6–10‰ BOD · antibiotics Mangrove loss

Shrimp and fish pond aquaculture in Honduras and Guatemala generates high-BOD effluent, elevated DIN and SRP from uneaten feed and waste, and antibiotic residues from disease management. The δ¹⁵N signature of aquaculture-sourced DIN is 6–10‰ — intermediate between karst (2–5‰) and agricultural (8–12‰), complicating attribution in zones where both sources are present. Critically, coastal aquaculture pond construction requires mangrove clearing, removing the alkalinity subsidy that buffers adjacent reef zones. CMIE must apply both the eutrophication prior elevation AND the TA deficit correction simultaneously when a reef zone is adjacent to both active aquaculture ponds and recently cleared mangrove.

Impact 06 · Maritime Operations
Cu antifouling Fuel hydrocarbons Ballast water DATA GAP

Antifouling biocides — particularly dissolved copper from copper-oxide based hull paints — suppress coral reproduction and calcification at concentrations of 3–10 µg/L, levels routinely found in marina environments. Fuel spills and chronic hydrocarbon seepage from vessel engines add additional organic loading. Ballast water discharge introduces non-native chemistry and biological material. Any MOOP station within 500 m of a marina or commercial anchorage must flag a dissolved copper and hydrocarbon data gap in the CMIE calibration record. Shamrocket operates with reef-safe antifouling methodology — a critical operational differentiator that removes the vessel itself as a chemical impact source during GROAN data collection.

When two or more sources overlap in δ¹⁵N range — particularly agricultural (8–12‰) and sewage (10–20‰), or aquaculture (6–10‰) and agricultural (8–12‰) — a multi-endmember isotope mixing model is required. Simple two-endmember attribution produces incorrect source fractions and will direct CMIE intervention to the wrong sector. The mixing model requires at least one confirmed endmember sample from each suspected source in the same reef zone catchment.

Zero-carbon attribution value. Shamrocket's zero-carbon, reef-safe operational methodology is not incidental to GROAN — it is data quality infrastructure. A diesel research vessel collecting corridor chemistry introduces copper antifouling leachate, fuel hydrocarbons, and exhaust-derived CO₂ enrichment within 500 m of every sampling station. Shamrocket removes the vessel as a confounding chemical source, allowing MOOP station data within marina and anchorage proximity to be interpreted without vessel contamination correction. This is documented in every CMIE calibration record as a methodological differentiator.

ES — Español

Los impactos humanos se traducen en química arrecifal a través de seis vías distintas, cada una con una firma química diagnóstica, un protocolo de atribución GROAN requerido y — en varios casos — una brecha de datos documentada que debe marcarse explícitamente en el registro de calibración CMIE. La distinción crítica entre esta lección y la taxonomía de estresores (L25) es la atribución: identificar la actividad humana responsable de una anomalía química. Un Puntaje de Decisión que nombra eutrofización como el estresor sin nombrar la actividad humana específica — agricultura, aguas residuales o acuicultura — no puede dirigir la intervención al sector correcto.

Agricultura: La carga de DIN pulsa en zonas arrecifales durante los eventos de escorrentía de la temporada lluviosa. La firma de isótopos de nitrógeno estable δ¹⁵N de 8–12‰ distingue el DIN agrícola del agua subterránea kárstica (2–5‰) y las aguas residuales (10–20‰).

Desarrollo Costero: Brecha de datos activa — el kit estándar MOOP no incluye análisis de metales pesados o hidrocarburos. Marcar en el registro de calibración CMIE para cualquier estación a 5 km de construcción activa o expansión urbana.

Turismo y Recreación: El oxibenzona y la octinoxata son disruptores endocrinos en corales a concentraciones tan bajas como 62 partes por trillón. Fuera del kit estándar MOOP — brecha de datos activa. Cualquier zona arrecifal que reciba >500 visitantes recreativos por año sin datos de oxibenzona u octinoxata debe marcarse como posiblemente subestimando el riesgo de blanqueamiento.

Aguas Residuales: La firma δ¹⁵N de las aguas residuales es 10–20‰. Las señales co-ocurrentes de aguas residuales y agricultura requieren un modelo de mezcla de δ¹⁵N de múltiples endmembers — la atribución simple de dos endmembers es insuficiente.

Acuicultura: La construcción de estanques de acuicultura costera requiere la tala de manglares, eliminando el subsidio de alcalinidad que amortigua las zonas arrecifales adyacentes. CMIE debe aplicar simultáneamente la elevación del prior de eutrofización Y la corrección de déficit de AT cuando una zona arrecifal es adyacente tanto a estanques de acuicultura activos como a manglar recientemente talado.

Operaciones Marítimas: Los biocidas antiensuciamiento — particularmente el cobre disuelto — suprimen la reproducción y calcificación coralina a concentraciones de 3–10 µg/L. Cualquier estación MOOP dentro de 500 m de una marina o fondeadero comercial debe marcar una brecha de datos de cobre disuelto e hidrocarburos. Shamrocket opera con metodología antiensuciamiento segura para arrecifes — un diferenciador operacional crítico.

Valor de atribución de cero carbono: La metodología operacional de cero carbono y segura para arrecifes de Shamrocket es infraestructura de calidad de datos. Un buque de investigación diésel introduce lixiviado de cobre, hidrocarburos de combustible y enriquecimiento de CO₂ del escape dentro de 500 m de cada estación de muestreo. Shamrocket elimina el buque como fuente química de confusión.

Application
EN — English
  • 01When elevated DIN is detected at a MAR reef zone station, do not attribute it to agriculture by default. Check for co-occurring sewage indicators (NH₄⁺ dominance, δ¹⁵N above 10‰) and aquaculture proximity. If two or more DIN sources overlap in the δ¹⁵N range — for example, co-occurring agricultural (8–12‰) and sewage (10–20‰) signals — apply a multi-endmember isotope mixing model before CMIE attribution. Simple two-endmember attribution in an overlapping zone produces incorrect source fractions and will direct intervention to the wrong sector.
  • 02When a reef zone is adjacent to both active aquaculture ponds and recently cleared mangrove, apply two simultaneous corrections in CMIE: the eutrophication prior elevation from pond effluent DIN/SRP loading, and the TA deficit correction from loss of mangrove alkalinity subsidy. These are not independent inputs to be processed separately — the aquaculture operation caused the mangrove clearance, and the compound effect on reef carbonate chemistry is the operational consequence CMIE must capture.
  • 03For any reef zone receiving more than 500 recreational visitors per year — including dive sites, snorkel tours, and swim beaches — and for which no oxybenzone or octinoxate data exists, document the data gap explicitly in the CMIE calibration record before generating a DS for bleaching risk. The absence of sunscreen chemical data means the DS underestimates bleaching risk at that site. This is not a minor caveat — oxybenzone causes bleaching at concentrations below the detection limit of standard colorimetric methods. The gap must be named, not assumed away.
  • 04Flag a dissolved copper and hydrocarbon data gap in the CMIE calibration record for every MOOP station within 500 m of a marina, commercial anchorage, or active port facility. Copper concentrations at levels that suppress coral reproduction are routinely found within 200–500 m of copper-antifouling vessel concentrations. The MOOP standard kit does not detect these concentrations. The gap is real, operationally significant, and must appear in the record before any DS is generated for reproductive health or calcification indicators at affected stations.
  • 05Document Shamrocket's zero-carbon, reef-safe operational methodology in every CMIE calibration record for corridor data. This distinguishes GROAN corridor samples from any data collected by diesel-powered research vessels and is relevant to data quality certification, funding body reporting, and future meta-analyses that attempt to pool GROAN data with other carbonate chemistry datasets. A vessel operating copper antifouling within 500 m of a sample station introduces a systematic chemical confound that cannot be retroactively corrected. GROAN data is free of this confound — that claim must be documented to be defensible.

Question 02
