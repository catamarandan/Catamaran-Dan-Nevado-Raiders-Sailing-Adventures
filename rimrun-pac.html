Pacific Circuit • Departs Oxnard, California • Mid-January 2027

Rim Run Pac

Shamrocket leaves Oxnard, California in the middle of January 2027, runs south to San Diego, then turns north up the Pacific coast to Washington State.

Oxnard • San Diego • California • Oregon • Washington • Pacific Coast

🇺🇸 Oxnard, California — Departure • Mid-January 2027

Shamrocket departs Oxnard on the Ventura County coast in the middle of January 2027. The Channel Islands lie offshore. This is the start of the Pacific circuit.

🇺🇸 Southern California — Coast and Channel Islands

From Oxnard the route runs south along the Southern California coast, past the Channel Islands and the Santa Barbara and Los Angeles shorelines, toward San Diego.

🇺🇸 San Diego — Southern Turning Point

The first southbound stretch ends in San Diego. From there the route turns north.

🇺🇸 California to Washington — Northbound Coast

Shamrocket heads north up the California coast, through Central and Northern California, along the Oregon coast, and on to Washington State.

🇺🇸 Washington State — Northern Limit of This Stretch

Washington State marks the northern limit of this first stretch of the Pacific circuit.

Why

Built to Impress One Person.

People ask who this expedition is meant to impress. The answer is one person — me. Not the sailing community. Not social media. Not institutions, sponsors, or strangers with opinions about what constitutes a worthy adventure. Three decades of work — on submarines, in boardrooms, on the water, and in the kind of places that reshape how a man sees the world — have produced a value set that doesn’t need external validation. The standard is internal. Always has been.

What that means in practice: the planning is meticulous, the preparation is relentless, and the execution is measured against one question — did I do this right? Not fast. Not impressively. Right. The sea is an honest judge and a poor audience. It doesn’t care about the narrative. It rewards preparation, punishes arrogance, and has no interest in anyone’s personal brand. That suits this expedition perfectly.

The highest loyalty here is to humanity itself — not to a tribe, a nation, a religion, a politics, or an ideology. The mission underneath every mile of this route is the advancement, protection, and survival of human civilization and the natural systems that sustain it.

This is not performance. It is purpose — built from the inside out, over thirty years, and now pointed at the water.

Turnagain Arm, Alaska — Where 4 Tides Was Born

4 Tides Cantina — The Onboard Bar

The Onboard Bar

4 Tides Cantina.

Every expedition needs a bar. This one was named at Turnagain Arm, Alaska — one of the most geologically dramatic stretches of water in the world, and the place where the Alaska to San Diego run found its rhythm.

Turnagain Arm got its name in 1778 when William Bligh — sailing master for Captain James Cook, later infamous for the Bounty mutiny — was sent to survey the inlet looking for the Northwest Passage. He had to turn around. Twice. He named it Turnagain in exasperation. The arm generates the largest tides in the United States — up to 40 feet — and twice a day those tides collide in the narrow channel and produce one of the largest tidal bore waves on earth: a wall of water up to 10 feet high, moving at up to 15 miles per hour, stretching the full width of the inlet. Local surfers ride it for miles. Harbor seals ride it in. Beluga whales follow.

Four tides. Two bores a day. The same water, returning. That's the bar. Named at the place where Cook had to turn around — because this expedition doesn't.

What It Is

The Pacific Circuit. Starting on the Coast.

Rim Run Pac starts in Oxnard, California, in the middle of January 2027. Shamrocket heads south to San Diego, then turns north up the Pacific coast to Washington State.

Same boat. Same sailor. Same methodology. The coastline is new, with its own weather, its own water and its own marine life.

The Start
Departure from Oxnard, California, mid-January 2027. First leg: Oxnard to San Diego, then north to Washington State. This is the full scope of the Rim Run Pac for now.
Why It Matters

One Coastline. Covered Stretch by Stretch.

The Pacific coast of North America runs from the warm waters of Southern California to the cold, green coast of Washington State. Shamrocket covers it one stretch at a time.
