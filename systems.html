DRAGR Expedition Sled

The DRAGR sled serves as the dedicated expedition cargo platform. It carries modular gear, provisions, recovery equipment, and cold-weather systems while preserving balance on the primary sailing platform.

Engineered with reinforced tow geometry and shock-absorbing bridle configuration, the sled distributes load safely while under sail. During overland transport it rides secured on the trailer, maintaining expedition readiness between launches.

EcoFlow Delta 3 Solar Power System

Portable, modular lithium power architecture providing silent, fuel-free onboard energy storage.

Recharged via flexible solar panels or standard AC when available, the system supports navigation electronics, communications, lighting, refrigeration, and auxiliary systems without dependence on combustion propulsion.

Torqeedo Electric Propulsion

High-efficiency 2.5 HP equivalent outboard with integrated 915 Wh high-performance lithium battery. Silent direct-drive system producing just 33 dB with instantaneous throttle response.

Solar-rechargeable via flexible panels or standard AC charging. Integrated onboard computer with GPS-based range calculation. Completely waterproof (IP67) with high-efficiency propeller.

Quechua 2-Second Expedition Tent

Rapid-deployment windproof and waterproof shelter used at anchor or ashore when conditions demand protection from rain, wind, cold, or heavy mosquito presence.

Provides fast environmental control during reef overnights, storm periods, or extended shore operations while maintaining expedition mobility.
