Blue Rim 5™

Expedition Systems

Practical systems for disciplined expedition sailing: energy, towing, navigation, tracking, safety, and guardian-vessel coordination.

Energy

Solar independence is the standard. Blue Rim 5™ uses an EcoFlow power system with dual 220W bifacial solar panels mounted on a lightweight stern/transom arch as a quick-release docking station.

  • EcoFlow modular power system sized to real daily loads.
  • Two portable 220W bifacial panels mounted in parallel.
  • Open/mesh support to preserve bifacial backside gain.
  • Anti-vibration measures, safety tethers, and protected quick-disconnect cabling.
  • Panels removable for shore deployment during beach/remote island camping.

DRAGR Sled + Towing Configuration

A dedicated expedition sled increases carrying capacity while keeping the sailing platform light and responsive. The towing system is designed to be overbuilt, load-distributed, and redundant.

  • Reinforced dual front tow points to prevent HDPE cracking under tension.
  • 15 ft straight-line working separation from cat stern hardpoints to sled.
  • Shock-absorbing bridle geometry with thimbles, sleeves, and chafe control.
  • Independent safety tether (always slack) separate from the primary bridle.
  • Crossbar hardpoints: 316 stainless padeyes + proper isolation and backing plates.

Navigation, Tracking & Risk Management

Precision replaces bravado. The expedition standard is short legs, coastal segmentation, and measured exposure windows. Long offshore runs are avoided unless unavoidable.

Blue Rim 5™ is supported by larger guardian vessels that add a second safety layer: weather intelligence, retrieval capability, spare parts, medical redundancy, and expedition logistics when conditions demand it.

  • Garmin inReach Explorer® satellite communicator for tracking and an independent SOS emergency response layer.
  • Two-way satellite messaging beyond cellular coverage.
  • LAT/LON waypoint discipline as standard routing practice.
  • Coastal routing emphasis with short-leg planning as the default.
  • Guardian-vessel coordination: comms plan, rendezvous logic, contingency support.
  • Redundancy by design so no single failure becomes a crisis.
  • No propane system onboard (intentional simplification and risk reduction).

Redundancy eliminates drama. Discipline preserves judgment.
