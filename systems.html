Systems & Platforms

Blue Rim 5™ operates on a sail-first philosophy supported by disciplined systems design. Every platform, component, and configuration choice reflects durability, redundancy, and simplicity over complexity.

DRAGR Expedition Sled

The DRAGR sled is a reinforced expedition cargo platform designed to carry provisions, camping gear, water containers, and modular systems without compromising vessel trim.

Engineered for load distribution and structural integrity, it is transported via a balanced tow bridle system and deployed at anchor for organized gear access.

EcoFlow Delta 3 Solar Power

Lightweight modular lithium power architecture providing silent onboard energy storage. Rechargeable via portable bifacial solar panels or AC when available.

Supports navigation electronics, communication systems, lighting, and expedition devices without reliance on fuel propulsion.

Torqeedo Electric Propulsion

High-efficiency 2.5 HP equivalent outboard with integrated 915 Wh lithium battery. Silent direct-drive system producing just 33 dB with instantaneous throttle response.

Solar-rechargeable via flexible panels or standard AC charging. Integrated onboard computer with GPS-based range calculation. Completely waterproof (IP67) with high-efficiency propeller.

Quencha 2-Second Expedition Tent

Rapid-deploy shelter system used at anchor when conditions demand wind protection, rain cover, or mosquito defense.

Windproof and waterproof construction allows cold-weather or wet anchorage comfort while maintaining expedition mobility.
