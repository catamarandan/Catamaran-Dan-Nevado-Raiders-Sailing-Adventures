Fleet Philosophy

Blue Rim 5™ operates under an inclusive fleet model. Small expedition sailboats form the core — agile, responsive, close to the water.

Larger guardian vessels travel with the expedition to add safety margin, retrieval capability, medical redundancy, weather intelligence, and logistics depth.

It is not small versus large. It is capability layered with resilience.
