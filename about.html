Expedition Philosophy

Blue Rim 5™ is not a casual cruise. It is a structured global sailing expedition built on repetition, systems discipline, daylight navigation, and conservative risk management.

Small expedition sailboats operate alongside guardian vessels when appropriate. Every coastal segment builds seamanship, cohesion, and operational maturity.

The mission is long-term: build a disciplined fleet capable of circumnavigation, while demonstrating that serious global sailing can be executed with lightweight, modular, solar-supported systems.
