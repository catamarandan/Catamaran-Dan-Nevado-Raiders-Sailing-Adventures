Beach Belly Beat Where California beach rock collides with the pulse of the Middle East. Acoustic beach vibes give way to darbuka, zills and an irresistible belly-dance groove as the first shimmy hits the sand. This is where the party starts. I’ll Have a Shimmy Shake The rhythm has taken hold. Beach rock gets hotter, the darbuka digs deeper, the hips get looser, and watching from the sidelines is becoming increasingly difficult. Playful, sexy and built to move, I’ll Have a Shimmy Shake turns the beach party into a dance floor. Shimmy the Shoreline The grand finale. No spectators. No sidelines. Surf guitar, pounding darbuka, zills, handclaps and a shoreline full of people become one enormous rhythm. What started as a beach beat has become something bigger — strangers dancing together, inhibitions disappearing, and the whole damn shoreline moving as one. You didn't come here like this. Neither did they.
