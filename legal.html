Proprietary Systems & Frameworks

Proprietary Systems & Frameworks

The following systems, frameworks, specifications, datasets, and architectural designs were developed independently by Nevado Ranch Camp LLC over approximately 18 months of self-directed field research and system design. Each constitutes protectable intellectual property under copyright law and/or trade secret law. No license, expressed or implied, is granted by publication of descriptive information on this website.

GROAN Decision System

Five-level cross-ecosystem ocean decision system. Full architecture, data pipeline, module design, interaction engine, and output framework. All 11 module specifications, 73-DTI data registry, and complete technical documentation are proprietary.

DMAP-CAL™ Decision Engine

22-segment mathematical decision framework: multi-objective Pareto optimization, Hamiltonian optimal control, Bellman dynamic programming, Kalman filtering, and reinforcement learning policy engine.

Cross-Module Interaction Engine (CMIE)

10-segment cross-ecosystem causal intelligence engine covering interaction matrix, nonlinear dynamics, graph-theoretic cascade networks, temporal and spatial dynamics, and uncertainty propagation.

GROAN Data Registry v2.0

73 registered Data Type Identifiers (DTIs) across 12 formally registered data sources. Normalization pipelines, confidence tier schema, edge compression protocol, and all associated JavaScript source files.

GSIN Technical Specification

21-segment seagrass intelligence module. Beer-Lambert light attenuation, Red Sea thermal calibration, nutrient cascade, GFW trawling detection, blue carbon tipping point detection, and full DMAP-CAL signal package.

GRIN Technical Specification

32-segment coral reef intelligence module. DHW threshold modeling, species-level symbiont physiology, genetic adaptation layer, digital twin, knowledge graph integration, and planetary-scale synthesis.

Image Confidence Tier Schema

Proprietary three-tier image quality classification for field data ingestion: High (>85%), Moderate (60–85%), Low (<60%). Confidence-weighted DMAP-CAL scoring and edge compression targeting protocol.

Rim Run™ Field Research Protocol

37-waypoint, 437–686 day field circuit methodology. Weather routing rules, MOOP hookah dive system integration, citizen science observer activation, and VoI-driven survey scheduling framework.

GROAN Curriculum

704-lesson bilingual (EN/ES) ocean science and decision systems curriculum. All lesson files, templating system, data tables, assessment frameworks, and progress tracking architecture are proprietary works of Nevado Ranch Camp LLC.
